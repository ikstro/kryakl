Kryakl /ˈkrjɑː.kəl/ beta · local AI for Windows 10 and 11

Talk to the duck (')>

For people who would rather run AI on their own computer than pay a cloud for it.

Kryakl is a small tray app (about 12 GB with its models :) for every program. It fixes text typed in the wrong layout, translates, reads English aloud, checks your English, types what you say, takes screenshots and records the screen. When you want to practise, it talks with you in English. One app instead of a layout switcher, a translator tab, a grammar checker and a screenshot tool.

Talk

Talk to the duck. It talks back.

Press your Talk key and say a line in English; press it again to send. The duck answers aloud, like a friend with time to chat. Your mistakes are marked under your own lines, with a short note in your language. The duck never corrects you: it answers what you meant.

  • Your level. A2, B1 or B2. The duck keeps to it: about 7, 8.5 or 15 words a sentence, measured.
  • Topics with a plan. Free talk, your day, a trip, a café, a job interview, your work. The interviewer asks one question at a time, then tells you how it went.
  • On your graphics card. The model Accurate uses (Gemma 4 E4B, 5.2 GB) answers in about a second on an RTX 2080 SUPER. It needs a card with 4 GB of its own memory. Speaking needs Kryakl's speech engine; typing works without it.
  • Your talks stay here. Kept on this computer for 30 days. Go on with one, or delete it.

e.g.PauseKeyboard layout

Typed in the wrong layout? Keep typing.

Kryakl fixes a word typed in the wrong layout as soon as you finish it, and switches the layout for the next one. It reads the keys of the word you are typing, never the text around it.

  • The fix key right after a fix takes it back and keeps the word as typed.
  • The fix key retypes the last words, or the selected text, in the layout that reads best. Again for the next one.
  • Learns as you go. A word you fix by hand twice is fixed always; a word of yours typed three times becomes yours.
  • Stays out of the way in password fields and full-screen games. NB and other abbreviations are left alone.
e.g.Shift×2Translate

Translate like DeepL. Paste it back.

Select a text in any program and press your key. The text and its translation open together; edit the text and it is translated again. Enter pastes the translation over your selection, and your clipboard stays as it was.

  • The direction is a rule. Your language goes into English, any other language into yours. 32 languages.
  • Three levels. Fast is Firefox's offline translator. Accurate and Most accurate run a Gemma 4 model on your own graphics card or processor.
  • Text on the screen. Ctrl+Alt+T, draw a box over a picture, a video or a game, and its text opens ready to translate.
Check my English

Write in English. The duck checks it.

Select what you wrote and press your Check key. Wrong words are struck out in red, the fix is in green, and one short note in your language explains the main mistake. A right text says so and stays as it is.

  • Real mistakes only. No rephrasing; code and names are left alone.
  • Measured on 44 texts, it caught 31 of 32 mistakes and changed none of the 11 right ones.
  • Your mistakes, kept. English › My mistakes shows the ones you repeat first.
Voice typing and recordings

Say it, and it's typed.

Press a key and speak. Kryakl types the phrase where the cursor is, in the language you spoke or straight in English. It recognizes while you talk, so the text is there a moment after the key.

  • Quick on an ordinary processor. 22 seconds of speech were typed 0.2 s after the key (Parakeet, on an Intel i9).
  • Transcripts of files, the microphone and Google Meet calls. Telling an hour's speakers apart takes about 2 minutes on an RTX card.
  • Names and terms. They come out written your way, in dictation and in transcripts.
  • Text editing. Off until you turn it on. Your local model cleans up a phrase before it is typed, or makes it a list, a business message or a task for an AI: about half a second on an RTX 2080 SUPER.
e.g.Print ScreenScreenshots and screen recording

Print Screen. Draw. Ctrl+C.

The screen freezes and you draw right on it: a frame, an arrow, a number, a blur over what nobody should see. Ctrl+C copies the picture with your drawings and the screen comes back. Switch to Video and the same frame records a window, a monitor or a piece of the screen.

  • Ten tools, one letter each. Frame, oval, arrow, line, pencil, marker, label, number, blur and pixels. The one you used last is in hand next time.
  • A blur that cannot be read back. Each block is replaced by its average with a little noise, so what you hid is gone from the picture.
  • Text from the picture. One button reads what is in the frame and opens it, ready to copy or translate.
  • Video with sound. Your microphone, the system's sound or both, into an MP4: 21 to 27 MB a minute at 1080p and 1440p. Nothing to download.
  • Clicks and zoom. Off until you turn them on. Rings show your clicks, and the video moves in where you click or press a key.
  • Panels inside a window. In programs with real panels, such as Total Commander, a click takes one list; the wheel goes wider.

Nothing you read leaves your computer.

Kryakl has no account, no cloud and no telemetry. The models download once and then work offline.

What you type

Stays here

Your talks with the duck

Stay here

The duck answers on your own graphics card. Talks are kept on this computer for 30 days, and what you write about yourself never leaves it.

Your voice and transcripts

Stay here

Recognized and edited on this computer. A compressed copy of the sound stays beside the transcript; the source file is never copied.

Your screenshots and recordings

Stay here

A screenshot is only what you copy or save. A recording goes into a folder of yours. Kryakl sends neither anywhere.

Your clipboard

As it was

Every copy Kryakl borrows is put back, and Windows' clipboard history never sees Kryakl's own copies.

Models and voices

Once

Downloaded when you first need them, from Mozilla and Hugging Face, each checked by size and SHA-256.

New versions

You decide

Kryakl never updates itself. You download a new installer from GitHub when you want it.

Three steps to the tray.

Free, open source, about a minute to set up. It installs over Krya, Altype and 2Talker and takes their words and settings along.

  1. 1

    Download the installer

    Get Kryakl-Setup.exe from the latest release on GitHub.

  2. 2

    Run it anyway

    The installer isn't signed, but its code is open on GitHub. Windows warns once: More info → Run anyway.

  3. 3

    Find the duck in the tray

    Kryakl starts with Windows. The first translation downloads your language pair once: about 58 MB for Ukrainian ↔ English.

What your computer needs

  • Any PC with Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Layout fixing, Fast translation, text from the screen, screenshots and reading aloud in a Windows voice. The natural voice wants 4 processor cores. Screen recording downloads nothing; so far it has been measured only on an RTX 2080 SUPER.
  • All of Kryakl: a graphics card with 4 GB of its own memory and about 12 GB of disk with every model. Accurate translation, Check my English, text editing and the talk with the duck run on it; speech on the card needs NVIDIA. Dictation and transcripts run on the processor too; telling speakers apart on the card wants 6 GB.
  • Most accurate: a graphics card with 9 GB of its own memory and 1.8 GB more of disk, about 14 GB in all.
