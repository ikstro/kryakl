## LinkedIn (EN)

Один пост: текст и ролик kryakl-en.mp4. Первая строка — то, что видно до «see more».

```text
I paid for two apps on my computer, and neither worked the way I wanted.

One was Everylang, a keyboard layout fixer; the other, WhisperTyping, for dictation. I also wanted to get better at English, which is very hard for me, and I didn't want more subscriptions.

My old PC under the desk, an i9 with an RTX 2080, was using about 20% of its power.

So I made Kryakl (')>, a local AI duck for Windows 10 and 11. My part was the scenarios and the rules; AI agents, Claude and GLM, wrote the code.

It is a small tray app (about 12 GB with its models :) that replaces four things: a layout switcher, a translator tab, a grammar checker and a screenshot tool. Everything runs on your computer until you connect the cloud Groq yourself. No subscription, no Kryakl account.

What it does:
1. Fixes text typed in the wrong keyboard layout.
2. Dictation on a hotkey, even from your language straight into English.
3. Call transcripts. A Chrome plugin turns a Google Meet call into text. An extra model tells who said what (a free Hugging Face account, once).
4. Translation in 32 languages. Select text, press a key, and one button swaps the original for the translation.
5. Screenshots and screen recording. Frame a window, the screen or a region; blur, label, read the text. The same frame records video with the mic or the system sound.
6. Check my English. Write in broken English, and the duck shows where you got it wrong.
7. Read aloud. Press Ctrl+C twice, and Kryakl reads the English aloud with the translation under it. Click a word for its meaning; a star saves it for Anki.
8. Talk to the duck. You speak, it answers aloud, and your mistakes are highlighted as you go. Levels A2, B1 or B2.

Gemma 4, Whisper and Parakeet run on your graphics card or processor. Open source (MIT), free.

A gaming PC is best: a laptop with an i7 and a GTX 1660 Ti runs it on medium; the most accurate models want 9 GB of graphics memory. No such PC? Connect the cloud Groq with your own key, and dictation, translation, Check my English and Talk fly on any laptop. Groq's free plan is enough for a working day.

I also tuned dictation by measurement: it now makes 6–9% fewer mistakes than before. I dictated the original of this post and fixed nothing. So it more or less works :)

ikstro.com/kryakl

#productdesign #buildinpublic #ai
```
